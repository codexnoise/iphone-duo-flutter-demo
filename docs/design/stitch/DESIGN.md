## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a museum-grade archival presence tailored for discerning music collectors, audio archivists, and cultural institutions. Its visual language merges the exacting rigor of Swiss modernist cataloguing with the dramatic luxury of mid-century editorial broadsheets. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- Deep, meditative dark mode environments that frame vinyl pressings, acetate masters, and high-fidelity artifacts like exhibition pieces.
- High-contrast, dramatic serifs delivering literary authority, counterbalanced by precision-cut grotesque sans-serifs for structured cataloguing metadata.
- A modernist art palette: deep gallery charcoals accented by ochre, vermilion, sage, and mint, applied with disciplined intention rather than ornamental noise.
- Generous, deliberate negative space punctuated by museum-style indexing rules, hair-thin division lines, and floating translucent glass strata.

## Colors
The palette balances low-reflectance, warm-charcoal gallery walls with purposeful pigments derived from modernist printmaking and archival notation.

### Surfaces & Foundations
- **Base Canvas (`neutral_color_hex`)**: `#1C1A19` — Deep warm charcoal canvas offering low eye fatigue and high optical contrast for album art.
- **Surface Level 1**: `#252220` — Secondary charcoal for side navigation rails, structural cards, and archival trays.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated)**: `#2E2A28` — Elevated cards, contextual drawers, and floating panels.
- **Hairline Border Token**: `#3A3533` — Exacting, hair-thin 1px division rules for layout bounding boxes and index grids.

### Modernist Accents
- **Primary Accent (`primary_color_hex`)**: `#2EC4B6` — Vibrant mint. Used for primary focus elements, play/scrub states, active catalog indices, and dynamic playback gauges.
- **Secondary Accent (`secondary_color_hex`)**: `#FFD166` — Warm ochre yellow. Reserved for limited pressing highlights, archival classifications, and mint-condition indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`tertiary_color_hex`)**: `#E76F51` — Deep vermilion/coral. Designates live recordings, rare bootlegs, master warnings, or critical collection actions.
- **Complementary Pigments**:
  - Deep Teal (`#0E3B43`): Structural fill for active states, badge undertones, and elevated deep-focus panels.
  - Muted Sage (`#8AB17D`): Provenance verification, matrix-runout annotations, and secondary archival classifications.

### Typography Shades
- **Editorial High Contrast**: `#F4F1EA` (Warm Alabaster) — Primary headlines, release titles, and principal values.
- **Archival Secondary**: `#A8A29E` (Muted Stone) — Track metadata, catalog prefixes, matrix codes, and secondary labels.
- **De-emphasized Rule/Muted**: `#6E6763` (Granite) — Timestamps, track numbers, and passive grid notations.

## Typography
Typography creates an intentional dialectic between high-contrast romanticism and neutral technical precision:

- **Bodoni Moda**: The voice of the artifact. Used exclusively for album titles, movement names, artist monikers, and grand exhibition headings. The high stroke contrast evokes editorial prestige and luxury auction monographs.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: The voice of the curator. A clean, disciplined grotesque sans-serif that renders matrix serials, catalog numbers, tracklists, technical fidelity tags (e.g., "180G MONO 33⅓ RPM"), and liner notes with legibility.
- **Labeling Protocol**: All `label-sm` and `label-md` tokens must be transformed to uppercase with extended letter-spacing (0.08em to 0.12em) to evoke archival ledger formatting.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column modernist grid engineered for high-density cataloguing and expansive editorial pauses:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Outer margin of `margin` (3rem / 48px), column gutters of `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Hero curation blocks span 8 or 12 columns, while split-screen track inspection drawers occupy 4-column sidebars.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Margin collapses to 2rem (32px), gutter fixed to 1rem (16px). Grids re-flow to double-column cards.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Margin snaps to `margin-mobile` (1.25rem / 20px), gutter snaps to `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem / 12px). Dense single-column vertical linear cataloging.
- **Archival Rhythm**: Generous negative space surrounding hero vinyl presentation (`space-xl`), contrasted with dense, metric data clusters spaced using `space-xs` and `space-sm` for track runouts, matrix codes, and index stamps.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids simulated three-dimensional drops in favor of architectural stratification, tonal layering, and fine optical glassmorphism:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#1C1A19` (Foundation plane).
  - **Level 1 (Structural Trays / Cards)**: `#252220` with a 1px solid `#3A3533` hair-thin boundary.
  - **Level 2 (Active Overlays / Dialogs)**: `#2E2A28` elevated via tonal change and an ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Museum Glassmorphism**:
  - Floating playback controllers, sticky audio headers, and hovering index filters utilize semi-transparent `#1C1A19` at 75% opacity with an intense backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), bounded along the bottom or perimeter by a `#3A3533` hairline stroke.
- **Border Treatment**:
  - No blunt or heavy strokes. All dividers, card boundaries, and grid splits must rely strictly on crisp 1px borders using `#3A3533`.

## Shapes
The system relies on crisp, tailored corners reflecting physical collector card cases and archival library drawers, tempered by select pill geometry:

- **Standard Elements (Corners = Soft / `0.25rem`)**: Album containers, catalog cards, popovers, and input fields adhere to a disciplined, low-radius edge (`0.25rem` / 4px). This preserves the sharp, geometric authority of mid-century book design.
- **Archival Pill Exceptions**: Category badges, catalog tags, state chips, and audio play-bars use fully rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) to create an immediate optical separation between administrative packaging and structural content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Archival Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#2EC4B6` fill with bold `#1C1A19` typography. Used for primary execution (e.g., "Add to Vault", "Initiate Playback").
- **Secondary / Exhibition Action**: Pill-shaped, transparent background with a crisp 1px `#3A3533` border and `#F4F1EA` text. On hover, background shifts to `#252220` and border illuminates to `#A8A29E`.
- **Text / Index Action**: Unbordered, tracking-wide uppercase typography (`label-md`) in `#A8A29E`, shifting to `#FFD166` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curatorial Badges**: Full pill shape, minimal height (24px), padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.
- **Variants**:
  - *Mint Pressing*: `#2EC4B6` text over a 15% opacity `#2EC4B6` tinted base.
  - *Archival / Rare*: `#FFD166` text over a 15% opacity `#FFD166` tinted base.
  - *Matrix / Condition Warning*: `#E76F51` text over a 15% opacity `#E76F51` tinted base.
  - *Format Class (e.g., "12\" 45 RPM")*: `#F4F1EA` text with a `#3A3533` hairline border and no background fill.

### Catalog Lists & Track tables
- **Layout**: Tabular museum ledger format. Each row features a subtle bottom hair-thin divider (`#3A3533`).
- **Columns**: Fixed numeric width for position ("A1", "A2", "B1") in granite `#6E6763` (`label-sm`), title set in high-contrast `#F4F1EA` (`body-md`), duration and matrix codes in muted stone `#A8A29E`.
- **Hover State**: Row transitions seamlessly to background `#252220`.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Curation Query**: Minimalist field with `#252220` background, 1px `#3A3533` border, and 4px corner radius. Text styled with `body-md` in `#F4F1EA`.
- **Focus State**: Hairline border shifts to `#2EC4B6` with zero fuzzy focus rings; focus is indicated purely by a crisp 1px stroke transition.

### Catalog Cards
- **Artifact Frame**: Surface `#252220` edged with a 1px `#3A3533` border. 
- **Structure**: High-resolution square cover art at the top, followed by an interior padded container with `Bodoni Moda` release title (`headline-sm`), artist name in `body-md` (`#A8A29E`), and an anchored bottom ledger row housing release year, catalog ID (e.g., "BST-81595"), and archival pill badge.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Matrix Runout Inspector**: Monospaced inspection panel utilizing 1px `#3A3533` borders on `#1C1A19` background for deciphering stamped vs. etched vinyl runout inscriptions.
- **Condition Grading Strip**: Segmented bar (M, NM, VG+, VG, G) featuring ochre `#FFD166` indicators against deep charcoal `#2E2A28` segments.
